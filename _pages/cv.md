Research experience
======
* 2021.10-2023.05: Simulation and Analysis of Complex Contagion in Social Networks
  * Used Python to develop a simulator based on Eckles’s work _Long Ties Accelerate Noisy Threshold based Contagions_ for calculating contagion time, introducing a new time discount factor and recalculating the utilities generated in the contagion.
  * Modifying the methods of calculating complex centrality produced by Guilbeault _Topological Measures for Identifying and Predicting the Spread of Complex Contagions_ under the time discount frame and calculating the utility generated in the frame.
  * Balancing the speed and extent of the spread by designing a mechanism between discount factors and simple contagions; Proposing a new way of seeding with the high new complex centrality generated by the time discount factor frame to maximize the utility.
  * Supervisor: Professor Amin Rahimian, Sociotechnical Systems Research Lab at Pittsburgh

* 2022.06-2022.08: Clustering Dynamics Network
  * Referred to the latest literature on clustering dynamic networks, including modifying the traditional Louvain clustering algorithm from static to dynamic (Dynamo algorithm), using a weighted algorithm to accelerate the speed of the clustering (LWEP algorithm), and employing a time decaying framework to combine cohesiveness and activeness by an activation function (Activation algorithm).
  * Utilized JAVA to reproduce relevant three dynamic clustering algorithms results.
  * Developed the visualization of the dynamic clustering results with the Pyvis module in Python.
  * Evaluated the results with metrics like NMI, Precision, Recall, F1 measure and Purity of algorithms.
  * Supervisor: Professor Hong Cheng, The Chinese University of Hong Kong

* 2023.01-2023.04: Hospital Discharge System Optimization(senior design project)
  * Analyzed the rationality of the flow of medical treatment according to the flow of patient visits.
  * Analyzed and predict the length of stay of patients in the hospital based on annual admission data.
  * Combined qualitative analysis to give comprehensive discharge recommendations and optimize the efficiency of different hospital consults.
  * Supervisor: Professor Caroline Kolman(PITT), Audrey Alcorn(UPMC Shadyside)
