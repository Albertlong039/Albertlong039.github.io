My name is Yixuan Long. I'm now studying a master of Operations Research at Georgia Tech and I am actively seeking a PhD position on the intersection of Operations Research and Healthcare!

I graduated from Sichuan University(B.Eng in Industrial Engineering, 2019-2021) and the University of Pittsburgh(B.S.E in Industrial Engineering, 2021-2023), with minors in Computer Science and Economics and a certificate in Engineering Data Analysis.

As an industrial engineering student, I have been always thinking about how  service people who really have needs with my knowledge. For me, IE is not only about chasing optimal and efficiency, which should also include the detecting the hidden problems behind people's lifes to make it better. Therefore, I am determined to work in the healthcare field, not only because it is interesting (of course, they are super interesting, which is actually the first motivation), but also because there is a huge scope for research at the intersection of operations research, AI technology and healthcare that need someone to explore. Whether it's using traditional engineering methods to improve the efficiency of hospital operations, analyzing and predicting chronic diseases, or applying the latest operations theories, AI techs to disease predictions and interventions; these problems are hugely appealing to me. At this stage I am working on a project to design a model to do screening, intervention for type 2 diabetes to improve the medical resources allocation. For the future, I am looking for research opportunities related to the prediction and analysis of chronic diseases, with a particular focus on the design of personalized treatment plans using statistical learning, reinforcement learning, and also the interpretability problems of these models.
